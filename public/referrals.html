THE FINE PRINT

Good to know

    QUESTIONS

    Answers

    Where do I find my code?

    At the top of this page while you are signed in. If it is not showing, ask the team at the lounge.

    My friend is already a member. Can I still refer them?

    No. Referrals are for new members, and the code is entered when they register.

    How do I know I have been paid?

    Your points balance goes up after your friend’s first exchange is complete. The team can check it for you at the lounge.

    Ready to refer someone?

    Send them to the registration page and remind them to use your code.
